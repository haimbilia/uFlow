# uFlow 2026.10.23.1

- Installed Wii U titles are discovered through privileged metadata mounts when ordinary filesystem access is denied, including USB storage with USB-as-SD setups. Original title IDs and launch paths are preserved.
- Installation waits for video shutdown and active artwork work to finish, then keeps background media stopped during installation to reduce competing I/O.
- Installation progress now shows elapsed time and average transfer speed, with clearer waiting and verification states.
- Successfully installed games are added to the library without requiring another scan.

Automatic update installs these app fixes. The updated **uFlow Setup** includes the same app for fresh installations and reinstalls; existing settings, games and saves are preserved. This release does not configure or change console boot files.

Hardware validation: internal-storage discovery was confirmed on the USB-as-SD setup. USB installation completed for Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga in about 7 seconds and New Super Mario Bros. U in about 2 minutes 49 seconds. NSMBU passed the earlier 23%/98% stalls and appeared in the library automatically. These are results on the tested console and SSD, not a guarantee of identical speed or a claim that every historical freeze is resolved.

Host validation covers installed-storage discovery, installation callbacks and failures, artwork worker shutdown, video lifecycle, signed updates, and the actual native installer payload for fresh installation and reinstallation.
